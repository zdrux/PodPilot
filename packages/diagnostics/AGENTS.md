# Diagnostics Workspace Guide

## Scope

Own deterministic diagnostic tools, evidence contracts, correlations, and runbook logic.

## Key Rules

- Every conclusion must identify supporting observations and their timestamps.
- Diagnostics must be testable without a live model.
- Bound queries by namespace, resource, time range, and result size.
- Prefer uncertainty or abstention when evidence conflicts or is incomplete.
